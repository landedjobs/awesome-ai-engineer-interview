## The loop

All stages below are from the [official process page](https://replit.com/interview-process).

1. **Recruiter interview**: your career path and goals; the role, Replit's mission and its operating principles.
2. **Hiring manager interview**: a deep dive into your experience and technical abilities against the role's expectations.
3. **Technical interview**: a live or take-home exercise, then a review with someone from the team. Formats named: project walkthrough, coding challenge, take-home assignment.
4. **Panel interview**: team members and stakeholders you'd work with; technical, problem-solving and behavioral skills.
5. **Offer.**

### 🔮 Representative follow-ups you should expect

*Not reported. Natural probes for an AI Engineer working on an AI coding agent product, given the stages above.*

- In the take-home review: *"Walk me through the part you'd rewrite with another day. Why?"*
- In the take-home review: *"Did you use AI tools on this? Show me a place where you overruled them."*
- Hiring manager: *"Describe an agent or LLM feature you shipped. How did you know it worked in production?"*
- Panel: *"Design the loop for an agent that edits a user's project, runs it, reads the errors and tries again. Where does it stop?"*
- Panel: *"A user says the agent broke their app. What do you log so you can replay what happened?"*

## What they're really testing

- **Real-world skill, not trick questions.** The page promises "No gotchas" and challenges similar to the actual work.
- **Defending your own work.** The technical step ends in a joint review, so you will be asked to explain choices in what you built.
- **How you think, create and collaborate.** Replit names all three on the page. The panel includes the people you would work with.
